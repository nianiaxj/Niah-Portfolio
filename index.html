Game project

Lavender's Run

A tiny browser game about quitting your desk job. Character, copy, concept, and code all mine.

The vibe I wanted. Late stage capitalism burnout as a soft little game. Escapist, a little funny, zero guilt.

The references I pulled. 2007 internet: MySpace-era pink profiles, Sanrio Town, pixel flash games, and old dress-up game screens. The web when it was pink and weird and felt like yours.

The choice that made it work. The collectibles are unread emails and the start button is your resignation. The joke is the mechanic. Corporate dread turned into something you collect and leave behind.

Hand-tracking art

Dandelion Wishes

Generative art that answers to your hand. Point, and flowers grow, move, and scatter particles into little poems. Concept, code, and poetry all mine.

The vibe I wanted. A garden you can touch through a screen. Slow, soft, and a little magical. No buttons or menus, just your hand and what grows from it.

The references I pulled. Webcam-toy-era internet. Flash games that used your camera for no reason other than joy. Flower fields, dandelion clocks, blowing seeds, and making a wish.

The choice that made it work. Gestures instead of clicks, and the flowers don't just bloom, they scatter into poems. Your movement becomes the interface and the reward is a line of poetry. The interaction is the writing.
